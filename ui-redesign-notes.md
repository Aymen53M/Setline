## Product and direction

- Product: Setline records training sessions and guides people through saved programs.
- Character: exact, grounded, quietly determined.
- World: a monochrome training logbook in graphite ink and paper white.
- Moments: a highly legible live recovery timer and a restrained strength chart built from real logs.
- Signature: a thin set-progress rail that marks completed sets through a workout.

## Applied principles

Keep the existing features, screen order and real exercise content. Use a single neutral ramp, three text roles, sentence-case copy, 400–600 weights, consistent 4px spacing, subdued hairline borders and restrained nested radii. Keep charts data-led with neutral series, remove decorative badges and glow effects, and use existing line icons consistently. Preserve timers as the active-workout focal point. Use honest empty states until the user saves workout history, and remove legacy seeded sessions. The interface chrome is monochrome; supplied exercise photographs remain real product content.

The visual audit found competing lime, blue, orange and lavender accents; tracked uppercase labels; overly heavy headings; inconsistent spacing and card radii; an over-colored multi-series chart; a decorative sparkle badge; and demo workout sessions that made first-run progress look like user activity.
